Frequently asked questions

What is Temps?

Temps is a direct-to-consumer shop from Kamrup Spice. You order pitha, laddoo, jalpan and rice on this site. We pack the order in Kamrup and ship it to your address.

Where can you get the products?

Order on this site. We ship across India. Delivery is ₹49, and free on orders of ₹499 and above.

What is there besides laddoo and pitha?

Jalpan, rice and grocery. That includes muri, akhoi, pithaguri, sandah, joha rice and atta.

Is doorstep delivery available?

Yes. Add a pack to the cart, enter your address at checkout, and we ship it to you.

Can children and older people eat these?

These are traditional snacks and rice foods. For an infant, ask a doctor before you feed them.
